Cookie Policy

Clear information about which cookies and similar technologies we use, why we use them, and how you can change your choice at any time.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Your choice matters

On this website, we use strictly necessary technologies for the proper functioning of the site. We only use Google Analytics cookies if you provide us with your prior consent.

You can change your choice at any time by clicking on the cookie icon in the bottom left corner of the page.

01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored by websites in your browser or on your device. Websites can use them to ensure basic functionality, save preferences, or measure website usage.

Besides traditional cookies, there are other similar technologies, such as browser local storage (localStorage). These technologies can also be important regarding rules on access to data stored on the user's device.

02

What do we use on this website?

Strictly necessary technologies

We use browser local storage to save your preference regarding analytical cookies. Without a saved choice, the site would prompt you for settings on every visit.

No analytics

Analytical cookies

Upon your consent, Google Analytics 4 is loaded. These cookies enable us to measure website usage and understand how visitors interact with individual pages.

Consent required
03

How does your consent work?

On your first visit, you can choose whether to allow the use of analytical cookies. The available options are "Accept all","Reject optional", and "Settings".

3
Change choice

You can turn off analytics at any time via the cookie icon.

If you do not allow analytical cookies, you can continue to use the website; rejecting analytics does not prevent normal access to content.

04

Changing or withdrawing consent

You can change your choice at any time. Click the cookie icon in the bottom left corner, open settings, select your desired option, and click "Save preference".

If you turn off analytics, analytical functionality will not load for future data collection. Changing settings is available without having to search for a specific link in the footer.

05

06

Data collected by Google Analytics

Google Analytics is an analytical service that helps us understand website usage. When used, data such as information about visited pages, time and manner of website usage, device and browser data, and other data necessary for analytics may be generated.

We only use Google Analytics with your consent. Details on data processing and options for managing data are also available in Google's privacy and Analytics policies.

07

Local storage and your choice

We use localStorage to store your preference. This is not a traditional HTTP cookie, but a web browser mechanism for local data storage.

Information about your preference regarding analytical cookies is stored so that the site does not have to display the prompt again on every visit.

08

09

What happens if you block cookies?

If you block or delete cookies and website data in your browser, your saved cookie preference may also be deleted. In this case, the website may prompt you to choose again on your next visit.

Blocking analytical cookies does not prevent normal use of the website. However, blocking all cookies or website data may affect functionality on some websites.

10

We may update this cookie policy from time to time if the operation of the website, the technologies used, or applicable legal requirements change. The date of the last update will always be indicated on this page.
